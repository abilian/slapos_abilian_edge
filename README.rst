SlapOS with Abilian SBE
=======================

Fork of `SlapOS <http://www.slapos.org/>`__ with the code to deploy
`Abilian SBE <https://github.com/abilian/abilian-sbe-monorepo/>`__.

About SlapOS
------------

SlapOS is an overlay operating system designed for managing distributed
POSIX-compliant infrastructures such as Linux and xBSD systems. It is
released as Free Software under a permissive license and is adept at
automating a wide range of cloud-based services—including IaaS, PaaS,
SaaS, and more—along with hyperconverged infrastructure, edge computing,
and network management for 4G/5G systems. SlapOS facilitates
comprehensive service management through automation of provisioning,
orchestration, monitoring, and other maintenance tasks across diverse
deployment scenarios, including data centers, edge nodes, and embedded
systems. The architecture of SlapOS is influenced by the concepts of
convergence and grid coordination, posited by Professors Mark Burgess
and Christophe Cérin, respectively. It features a minimalistic recursive
structure with two main components: the SlapOS Master, which maintains a
common state across the infrastructure, and the SlapOS Node, which
autonomously ensures each node’s state aligns with the master
configuration.

About Abilian SBE
-----------------

Abilian SBE is an open-source enterprise social networking software
designed to enhance communication, collaboration, knowledge management,
and productivity within organizations. It serves as a multi-functional
platform that supports the development of enterprise social networks to
foster internal communication, collaboration on projects, and efficient
knowledge sharing. By facilitating real-time interaction and reducing
reliance on emails, Abilian SBE helps streamline processes and enhance
the overall corporate culture, allowing employees to better engage with
the company’s mission and values. The software includes features for
real-time discussions, project management, document sharing, and
customization of the work environment to suit individual preferences,
alongside robust security measures to protect sensitive corporate data.
This comprehensive suite of tools provided by Abilian SBE significantly
contributes to improving employee productivity and managing corporate
knowledge effectively, making it a valuable asset for modern businesses
aiming to leverage social technology for organizational success.
